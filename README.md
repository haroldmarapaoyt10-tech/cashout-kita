# 💸 Cashout Kita

A simple, free, and offline-ready cash-out transaction recorder. Designed for personal or small business use to track cash-outs, calculate service fees, and generate reports without the need for an account or internet connection.

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Active-success) ![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Free-blue) ![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-Web%20%7C%20Mobile-orange)

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Dashboard:** Quick view of today's and all-time transactions, total cash-outs, and service fees.
- 🧮 **Smart Fee Calculator:** Customizable fee percentage (default 1%) with automatic rounding up to the nearest ₱5.
- 🛡️ **Live Duplicate Checker:** Real-time validation for Reference Numbers to prevent duplicate entries.
- 📑 **Smart Pagination:** Customizable rows per page (5, 10, 15, 20, 50, 100) for easy navigation of long transaction histories.
- 📈 **Reports:** Filter and view transactions by Today, This Month, or a Custom Date range.
- 💾 **Data Management:** Easily Export and Restore your data via JSON backup files.
- 📱 **Offline-Ready & PWA:** Works without an internet connection once loaded. Add to your Home Screen for a native app-like experience.
- 🔒 **Privacy First:** No accounts, no activation, and no external servers. Your data stays securely in your browser's local storage.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Access the App:** Open the web link in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge).
2. **Add to Home Screen (Optional):** For a seamless experience, open the app in your mobile browser, tap the menu (⋮), and select **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app"**.
3. **Record a Transaction:** Fill in the Reference Number, Cash-out Amount, and Date. The fee will be calculated automatically.
4. **Backup Your Data:** Go to the **Data Management** section and click **Export Backup (JSON)** regularly to keep your data safe, especially before clearing your browser cache.

## 🔒 Data & Privacy

**Cashout Kita** does not use any cloud database. All your records are stored locally in your device's browser (`localStorage`). 
- Your data is 100% private and stays on your device.
- If you switch devices or clear your browser data, make sure to **Export Backup** first and **Restore** it on your new device.
